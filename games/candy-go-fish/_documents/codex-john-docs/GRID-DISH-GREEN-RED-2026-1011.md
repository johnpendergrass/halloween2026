## Prompt

Recolor this plain rounded rectangular ceramic grid dish into Wendy's muted GREEN AND RED palette. Keep geometry, rim thickness, shallow directly overhead perspective, realistic glossy ceramic material, transparent exterior and whole-object silhouette unchanged. Width:height about 1.26:1; final export 300x238 transparent PNG. Interior is muted sage/forest-green ceramic glaze with a few restrained darker green mottled areas. Rim is a soft pale sage/green-cream with a narrow muted brick-red / cranberry-red accent edge. Keep GREEN the dominant color and RED a restrained accent. No yellow or orange palette; no neon or saturated Christmas-like stripes. Uncluttered, low saturation, legible beneath future candy illustrations. No characters, animals, witch, cat, ghost, candies, numbers, lettering, badges or extra objects. One empty plain ceramic dish only. Match the human brown/yellow tray's exact style and proportions, but clearly green/red. Do not add a setting/background/table or cast shadow outside the silhouette.
