Character portraits - the originals (moved here 2026-10-16, John + Claude)

These six PNGs were the live portraits in assets/opponent-icons and
assets/player-icons until 2026-10-16. The game now uses 512 x 512 WebP
copies of them (same file names, .webp) made from these files with
Pillow: longest side 512, LANCZOS resample, WebP quality 85.

WHY
A cold start of the game (empty browser cache, as every friend's first
visit is) downloaded about 12 MB, and these six files were 8.8 MB of it:
HERMIONE 2.0 MB, RON 1.9, HARRY 1.8, sully 1.4, mike 1.0, boo 0.6. On a
good 4G connection they alone blew past the six-second preload bound,
so "Let's play" could not appear before about 6.4 seconds.

The portrait is drawn as a circle 22.5% of the frame width. The frame is
capped by screen height, so the largest the portrait is ever drawn is:
  iPhone (3x)              ~258 device px
  iPad 12.9 portrait (2x)  ~311 device px   <- the biggest real case
  1080p monitor            ~123 device px
  4K monitor               ~246 device px at any Windows scaling
  8K monitor               ~492 device px
512 px covers all of them. The 1024 px originals were never shown at
more than a third of their size.

RESULT
Six WebP files, about 180 kB in total (fifty times smaller), with the
transparent backgrounds of the three opponent portraits kept. WebP is
shown by every browser that runs this game (iOS 14+, Chrome, Edge,
Firefox, Safari).

If a portrait ever needs redoing, start from these originals (or from
_documents/design ideas/opponents and players, where the earlier
candidates live) and make a new 512 WebP the same way.
