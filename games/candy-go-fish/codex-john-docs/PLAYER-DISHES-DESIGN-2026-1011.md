Output one wide shallow RECTANGULAR plate/tray with gently rounded corners, width:height exactly 20:11 (about 260 x 143 pixels for final export). Straight long sides and short sides with softly rounded corners; not an oval and not a circular plate. Directly overhead view, minimal perspective, bright ivory glazed rim edged with a simple thin pumpkin-orange stripe; muted mottled sage-green/orange interior glaze. Match the reference's photorealistic ceramic highlights, tactile glaze, bright rim and uncluttered design. Center decoration: ONE friendly Halloween WITCH appearing to climb or peek out of the dish, hands gripping the back/top rim, large recognizable black pointed witch hat, simple readable face, dark purple clothing. Make this a charming painted/low-relief ceramic decoration, not a real person in a room. Her shoulders/body remain in the dish; hat/head/arms can slightly overlap the top rim without making the asset much taller. Keep a WIDE silhouette that fits the short tray height and reads clearly when rendered at about 68x37 CSS pixels. Fill most of the wide canvas with the tray, with a small transparent margin around every edge. Clean very simple rim: no floral motifs, dots, vines, lettering or repeated decoration. NO candies, NO wrappers, NO text, NO number badge, NO table, NO external backdrop, NO extra objects, no separate ghost. Transparent outside the tray/character. Whole object fully visible, clean edge, no clipped rim. The character and glaze are decoration ON the tray; this empty tray will later hold generic candy illustrations.
