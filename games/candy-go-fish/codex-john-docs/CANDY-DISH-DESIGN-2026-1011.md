## Prompt

Create ONE photorealistic Halloween candy dish as an isolated game asset, approximately 300 x 300 pixels, square canvas. Transparent background outside the object. The dish itself is a shallow round ceramic plate-like candy dish, NOT a deep bowl. View it from directly above with a tiny amount of dimensional depth at the rim, so the decorated interior is plainly visible and it reads as a plate at small phone size. Smooth glazed ceramic with realistic subtle reflections, gentle natural studio lighting, crisp clean silhouette. A relatively bright cream/ivory rim with restrained warm orange accents gives a clear visible edge against a dark green game background. In the center is one friendly simple WHITE GHOST decoration painted onto the ceramic glaze, with dark little eyes, on a MUTED sage-green and subdued pumpkin-orange interior glaze/design. The ghost is a printed/painted design, not a three-dimensional character, and must remain legible at 300px. Halloween themed, tasteful and realistic, no lettering or logo. A little handmade ceramic texture is okay but not busy. Let the plate occupy about 92% of the square, fully visible with a narrow transparent margin around every edge. No candies, no wrappers, no sweets, no utensils, no tabletop, no scene, no external backdrop, no hands, no mockup, no second plate. This is an EMPTY decorative plate; generic candies will be designed separately later. Keep its shallow plate shape, relatively bright rim, central friendly ghost, and muted green/orange interior as the main features.
