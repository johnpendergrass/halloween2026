# Current rules and interaction

Updated October 11, 2026. [Current handoff](CONTINUATION-2026-1011.md) takes precedence.

Ten types, three copies each, five candies initially per player; first actor is random. Request a held type; success moves one candy and keeps the turn. Three matching candies are automatically removed as a set. Initial sets skip the later earned-set popup.

Fishing draws one candy and currently always ends the turn. Good Fish (drawn set retains the turn) is an approved earlier proposal, not implemented. No refill. End on empty dish, all sets, or empty active hand, in that reason priority. Collect a possible final-draw set before ending. Earned final sets celebrate before game-over results. Most sets wins; ties are possible.

The human selects a held candy. Its highlight clears once the question sends, after one second or a question tap. The game knows both answers and displays them automatically; the human never chooses truth/falsity.

Either YES answer keeps both boxes for four active seconds, then clears them and transfers automatically. Either text box or the Candy Dish shortcuts that wait. When Wendy asks for a held candy, tapping the matching slot at any point gives immediately and interrupts speech/waits. Other slots remain disabled.

Either NO answer retains question and reply until the human clicks either box or the Candy Dish. Exactly one candy is drawn for the requester, with an unidentified wrapper for Wendy's private draw. There is no NO timeout.

Accepted actions lock controls immediately; repeated clicks cannot repeat a move. Dialogs pause/lock; new deals invalidate old asynchronous work. Scores are completed human sets, reported once through the helper. Opponent strategy receives only its hand and public observations, never private human cards or dish order.
