Keep the SAME overall dish dimensions and footprint, fully visible on a square transparent canvas, intended for a 300x300 game asset. Simplify it substantially: remove the busy leaves, vines, flowers, dot clusters and repeated patterns from the rim and interior. Use a mostly plain bright ivory rim with a single clean thin orange accent band and an uncluttered softly mottled muted green/orange glazed interior. Make the central friendly white ghost approximately 50 pixels wider and taller on the 300px final asset (roughly 165-175px wide instead of about 115-125px wide). Give the ghost an energetic escaping/climbing pose: head raised toward the top rim, both little arms reaching toward or hooking over the inner rim, wavy lower body lifting as if it is about to float out of the dish. The ghost remains a charming ceramic decoration / painted low-relief character belonging to the dish, not a separate hovering person. A subtle low relief or shadow at the arms can suggest it is climbing out. Keep recognizable dark eyes and a friendly expression. Large clear silhouette that reads at small phone size. Keep the whole dish in frame and avoid adding extra elements outside its silhouette. No candies, no wrappers, no text, no table, no background. This is version 2: less patterning, a larger more animated ghost; keep the photograph-like ceramic material and restrained green/orange color.
