## Preparation and playback

js/preload.js centralizes fixed/configured image inventory, image loading/decoding, audio preparation and spinner-tick buffer calculation. sound.js owns playback and the UI/music gain buses.

Since October 16 (Option C of claude-john-docs/claudePlans/iphone-speech-buffers-plan.md) there are two kinds of sound:

- UI clips and speech are DECODED BUFFERS. At startup sound.js fetches the twelve short MP3s (confirmText, the two set fanfares, the three character cues, the six goes-first speeches) and decodes each with decodeAudioData into an AudioBuffer kept in a Map by sound key. play(sound) makes a throwaway AudioBufferSourceNode through a per-play gain into the UI channel, exactly like the spinner ticks, and returns a "voice" ({ sound, duration, gain, onended }). The 36 victory speeches and the tie are NOT decoded at startup: scheduleVictory decodes the one it needs (prepareSpeech) and plays it when the two-second timer and the decode are both done; the buffer is cached for the replay button. A failed fetch or decode is forgotten so the next play() tries again. Why: on John's iPhone every media element was unreliable (a clip could only start inside a tap, and sometimes not even then), while the ticks, which already played from a buffer, always worked. The decoded sounds need HTTP: Chrome refuses fetch() over file://, so a page opened straight from disk has music but no UI sounds or speech.
- The three soundtracks stay MEDIA ELEMENTS (minutes long; a decoded copy would be tens of MB each), routed through the music gain. They are only ever asked to play inside a tap, and CandySound.userTapped(), called for every click on the page, retries a tune that is wanted but not playing (a start asked for before the file was ready stalls on iOS, which then shows the tune as "paused" in Now Playing). The loading-finished auto-start was removed: nothing starts the music without a tap.

Startup (changed 2026-10-16 afternoon after John's cold-start test: ten seconds of "Loading assets" on wifi): "Let's play" waits only for the images and the twelve short sounds (six-second bound each). Only the CHOSEN tune is prepared at startup, after the saved settings are restored, and the button does not wait for it (CandyPreload.whenMusicReady() tracks it, 20-second bound, failures recorded). The other two tunes are created, and start downloading, when picked in the Sound popup (selectTrack). If the tune is not there yet when the artwork is tapped, the next tap retries it. Failures permit play. Browser preload/readiness behavior varies by device.

Spinner: synthesized 18ms noise tick, softened by a 3.2kHz low-pass, 50% gain. First tick begins with rotation, then roughly every 60 degrees follows the actual plate speed with a minimum 28ms gap. Animation pause/mute/exit/reduced motion are handled. No spinner confirmation effect has been added; goes-first speech plays at landing.

Earned sets: human role uses winsSet-player; wendy/opponent role uses winsSet-opponent. Each plays once when its set dialog appears. The synthesized trumpet trial was removed.

Audio wake-up: phones keep the Web Audio context suspended until a tap resumes it; CandySound.unlock() does that from every click on the page (userTapped), the dialogs' confirm button and the first dish tap (spinner ticks). Once awake, the engine plays decoded buffers whenever the game asks, so the timer-started goes-first and victory speeches work on the phone. Victory: speech starts after two seconds, BIGWIN for a margin of at least three sets, CLOSE for one or two, the tie voiceover for a tie; missing clips stay silent. Replay restarts immediately from the cached buffer. New game, UI/global mute and page exit cancel pending/playing speeches (a speech still decoding when cancelled never plays late); rain drain disables replay.

## Music and controls (2026-10-15, Claude)

Welcome, Deal again? and Rules share the soundtrack. The chosen tune loops and fades in over three seconds. In the default play mode, When paused, closing/confirming/canceling a dialog fades it to zero over two seconds while gameplay proceeds, then pauses preserving currentTime; reopening resumes from there. In Continuous mode the dialog closing changes nothing and the tune keeps playing through the game. Each tune is its own media element, so switching away and back resumes that tune where it left off. A fresh page load starts every tune at zero.

Welcome action shows Loading assets until preparation settles, then Let's play. The music starts only from a tap on the welcome artwork (pointerdown and click are both listened to; on John's iPhone pointerdown alone worked only sometimes); there is no auto-start. Let's play remains a single-click entry to the game. Other dialogs open from a user gesture.

One SOUND speaker button sits at the top right of these three dialogs only, drawn with John's speaker photo (assets/buttons/speaker-grill.png) and the bold label SOUND below it; a red slash crosses it when both channels are off. It opens the Sound popup (2026-10-15 afternoon; it replaced the separate UI toggle and the Music popup). The popup is a second overlay with the welcome's vine frame, laid out after John's sketch: UI and MUSIC speaker toggles side by side (steady green glow when on, red slash when off); then MUSIC SOUNDTRACK: Soundtrack with left/right arrows around the tune name (Earth, Haunted, Wonderful), When to play as a two-way slider switch (Always or Paused), and a Volume slider with fixed stops 0, 20, 40, 60, 80, 100 and tick numbers under it. Changes apply at once so they can be heard. CANCEL restores all five settings as they were when the popup opened; OK keeps and saves them (the layout followed John's second sketch on the same day: speakers centred one above the other, no labels on the three controls). The UI OFF switch only flips the channel flag (the channel's gain goes to zero), cancels a pending victory speech and pauses a clip that is actually playing; idle clips are left alone, because pausing all 84 of them took seconds on an iPhone, and each clip rewinds itself when played. Game over (John, 2026-10-15): in Paused mode the soundtrack ramps up about a second after the victory speech ends (a fallback timer of the clip's length plus a second covers a blocked clip; three seconds when there is no speech at all) and plays until New game is tapped, when it fades during the candy rain. In Always mode nothing changes because it is already playing.

Switching tunes fades the old one out over one second (keeping its position) and fades the new one in. Music OFF pauses immediately, preserving position; ON resumes with the fade-in. UI OFF stops effects and pending victory speech but leaves music alone.

Volume: the slider value is the music channel volume (0 to 1). sound.js scales the music channel by 1/0.75, so the level the game first shipped with (clip gain 0.5 at channel gain 1) now sits at slider 75 and the top stop is a third louder. The default stop is 80. Per-sound gain stays 50%; the UI channel stays at gain 1.

Remembered settings: Done in the popup, and the UI toggle, write one note to localStorage under the key halloween2026:candy-go-fish:music: { enabled, volume, track, mode, ui }. It is read once at startup, every value checked, and a missing or damaged note is ignored. This is allowed by rule 21 of the game requirements (relaxed 2026-10-15); the shell's own halloween2026:candy-go-fish note is never touched. Device volume remains user-controlled; the shell Sound switch has no game API.

APIs in sound.js: musicTracks, currentTrack, selectTrack(id), musicMode, setMusicMode('paused'|'continuous'), setChannelEnabled, setChannelVolume, getChannel, prepareWelcomeMusic (returns the chosen tune's clip), startWelcomeMusic, retryMusic, userTapped, fadeOutWelcomeMusic, prepareAssets (returns [{ url, ready }] for the twelve startup decodes), prepareSpeech(sound), decodedSounds, play(sound) (returns a voice) and global setEnabled.

The Music popup itself was checked on 2026-10-15 with two throwaway Playwright scripts (tune switching with position kept, Cancel/Done, Continuous surviving Let's play and Rules, settings restored after a reload, damaged note ignored); they were not kept. Chrome checks do not replace physical iPhone listening/autoplay review. Current cache stamp: 2026-1016-fast-start.
